# Activity 5: Exploring and Improving AI Prompts

This activity focused on creating an initial AI prompt in **Gemini** to upscale and restore a group webcam photo, refining the prompt into a detailed cinematic neo-noir specification, and comparing both generated outputs in terms of clarity, detail, accuracy, and usefulness.

- **Submission File:** [CERMINO_ACTIVITY5.pdf](CERMINO_ACTIVITY5.pdf)

## What Have I Learned?

- **Key Concepts & Skills:** I learned how prompt specificity—including lighting, style, composition, and identity-preservation constraints—dramatically changes the quality and direction of AI-generated outputs.
- **Challenge & Solution:** Getting the AI to apply heavy stylization without altering the subjects' facial identities was a key challenge; I solved this by explicitly instructing the model to preserve facial features, glasses, and positions while prioritizing identity preservation over stylization.
- **Future Application:** I can apply iterative prompt engineering to produce clearer, more accurate results when using AI tools for academic research, documentation, and visual communication.
